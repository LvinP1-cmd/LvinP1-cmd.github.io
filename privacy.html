Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Review this policy against the exact SDKs and Play Console Data safety answers in your final production release.

Overview

Luminaries is a Catholic prayer and learning application. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, improve, secure, support, and monetize the app.

Information stored on your device

Luminaries may store preferences and app activity locally on your device, including favorites, saved items, onboarding status, reminder settings, prayer progress, and similar app state.

Advertising

Luminaries uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in some areas of the app. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising information, approximate location derived from network information, and other data as described in Google's privacy and advertising documentation. Where required, Luminaries uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

Firebase and app services

Luminaries may use Firebase or related Google services for app infrastructure. The exact information processed depends on the Firebase features enabled in the released version of the app.

Notifications

If you enable prayer reminders, Luminaries requests notification permission and stores the reminder settings needed to schedule those notifications. You can disable reminders in Luminaries or through your device settings.

Third-party services

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Depending on the released build, these may include Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Firebase, Google Play services, Expo, and related infrastructure providers.

Children

Luminaries is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If the app is later specifically directed to children or families, the policy and data practices should be reviewed before release.

Your choices

Depending on your location and device, you may be able to manage advertising privacy choices, reset advertising identifiers, disable notifications, clear app data, or uninstall Luminaries.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Luminaries changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the details on the Support page.
